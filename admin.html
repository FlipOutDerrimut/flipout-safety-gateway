Steps to locate your Access Token natively inside the Deputy interface:
  1. Log into your primary Deputy Administrator account.
  2. Click your Profile Initials / Avatar in the top right corner of the screen.
  3. Select System Settings from the dropdown menu selections.
  4. Click on Integrations located within the left-hand sidebar workspace panel.
  5. Select the Developer Applications option grid.
  6. Click New Application, define a name (such as Safety Trapdoor), and click Save to retrieve your permanent access token string.
