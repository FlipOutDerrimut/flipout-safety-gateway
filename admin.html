Steps to generate your token natively inside Deputy:
  1. Open your Deputy Administrator profile dashboard.
  2. Click your Profile Icon or Initials located at the top-right corner.
  3. Select System Settings from the available drop-down links.
  4. Click on Integrations within the left-hand navigation sidebar panel.
  5. Open the Developer Applications management pane.
  6. Click New Application, choose a reference name (e.g., Safety Trapdoor), and save to extract the key.
